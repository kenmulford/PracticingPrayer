Shared prayers now survive an install, and importing skips what you already have.

• Got a shared prayer link before you had the app? Install, open Practicing Prayer, and tap Import to bring it in.
• Importing into an existing card skips requests already on it. Tap "+ Add" to bring one back.
• While your protected prayers are locked, the import card picker hides or masks them.
• Plus bug fixes and reliability improvements.
