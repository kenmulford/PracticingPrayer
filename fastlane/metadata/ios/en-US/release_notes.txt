Protect your most personal prayers with Face ID, Touch ID, or a PIN.

• New: mark any prayer card or collection as Locked (content hidden until you unlock it) or Hidden (masked in your lists) for an extra layer of privacy.
• Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or a 6-digit PIN.
• Protected prayers are excluded from Prayer Time and search until you authenticate, and can't be shared while locked.
• The app automatically locks protected content when you switch away, even mid-session.
• Backups now keep protected prayers safe in a separate encrypted file.
• New "Show Confidential" option lets you reveal protected prayers on the Prayers list.
• Accessibility: the Home dashboard now reflows to a single column at large text sizes so your summary counts stay visible.
